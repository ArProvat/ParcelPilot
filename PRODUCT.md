# ParcelPilot Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Goal

ParcelPilot empowers logistics teams and end-users with an AI assistant that handles parcel tracking, policy inquiries, automated credit calculation, ticket resolution, and guided human-in-the-loop approvals.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Intelligent Tracking & Order Lookup**: Query real-time statuses, carrier details, and estimated delivery dates.
2. **Authoritative Knowledge Base**: Ingest SOPs, carrier guidelines, and SLAs with clear hierarchy to prevent hallucinated or outdated policy responses.
3. **Automated & Guardrailed Actions**:
   - Issue service credits within pre-approved thresholds.
   - Escalate severe disputes or high-value credits for human review.
   - Process cancellations strictly following SLA time windows.
4. **Auditability & Traceability**: Full provenance linking each agent decision back to source documents and database records.
